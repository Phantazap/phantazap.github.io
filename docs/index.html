This is Laioxy's website (Translated to English by Phantazap)

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time, Darkness, and Sky.

  • Wondermail Generator

    Generates custom Wondermail codes with special parameters.
    You can make missions with valuable rewards, missions with high difficulty, or pretty much anything you can think of. However, it can also be dangerous if used incorrectly, so use at your own risk.

    • Force Unlock Dungeon Exploit (Sky)

      Forcibly unlock most dungeons in the game.
      This includes dungeons that normally can not be accessed. (Exclusive to Explorers of Sky)

    • Any Species Egg Exploit

      Obtain any Pokémon species by using eggs.
      Nearly every Pokémon can be obtained this way, from regular Pokémon, to legendaries, to Pokémon you were never intended to have!

  • SOS Duplication

    A tool for duplicating SOS Missions to be played repeatedly.

  • A-OK Generator

    Generates an A-OK code to rescue a team from a dungeon. You may also attach an item.
