Any Species Egg Exploit Wondermail Generator

A tool to generate Wondermail for hatching any Pokémon in "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time, Darkness, and Sky"
This generator is simplified specifically to do this exploit.

The content on this page is based on Explorers of Sky, so data may differ for Pokémon that are not in Explorers of Time/Darkness, such as Shaymin.

Please make sure you meet the following requirements before continuing:

  • Treasure Bag does not contain a Gabite Scale
  • Chansey's Day Care does not already have an egg
  • Labyrinth Cave is unlocked
    • Chapter 10 must have been completed
    • In Time/Darkness, Labyrinth Cave must be unlocked via Wondermail. In Sky, you can also recieve its mission from a Togetic in Spinda's Cafe.

After accepting the mission generated by this tool, clear Labyrinth Cave. You will recieve an egg that, when hatched, will be the species you choose here.
